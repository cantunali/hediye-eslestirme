# Crimson Modern Design System

## Brand & Style
Crimson Modern is a design system built for high-impact, modern digital experiences. It transitions away from rigid industrialism toward a more passionate, energetic, and approachable aesthetic. The brand evokes a sense of urgency, precision, and sophistication, making it ideal for dynamic platforms that require both professional reliability and a bold visual presence.

The style is characterized by:
- **Modern Corporate:** A balanced blend of professional structure and contemporary flair.
- **Vibrant Energy:** Using deep reds and teals to create a high-contrast, memorable visual identity.
- **Geometric Clarity:** Utilizing clean lines and geometric typography to ensure legibility and a tech-forward feel.
- **Soft Precision:** Moving from sharp corners to rounded forms to feel more inclusive and ergonomic.

## Colors
The palette is anchored by a vibrant red seed, branching into a range of professional tones that balance intensity with clarity.

- **Primary (#eb1237):** A powerful, high-chroma red used for primary actions, branding, and highlighting key information.
- **Secondary (#d0474c):** A muted, softer red-rose used for supporting elements and varied UI states without overwhelming the user.
- **Tertiary (#008494):** A sophisticated deep teal that provides a cooling contrast to the primary reds, perfect for accents and specialized data visualization.
- **Neutral (#8c706f):** A warm, slightly desaturated grey used for surfaces, borders, and text to maintain readability while complementing the warm primary palette.

## Typography
The system utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels. This geometric sans-serif offers a modern, clean look with excellent legibility and a slight rhythmic bounce that fits the new, softer brand personality.

- **Headlines:** Set with high weight (700) and tight tracking for a strong, impactful editorial feel.
- **Body:** Optimized for readability with a standard 1.5x line height and normal weight.
- **Labels:** Slightly tighter and sometimes uppercase to provide clear distinction for metadata and small UI indicators.

## Layout & Spacing
The design follows a fluid grid system built on an 8px base unit. This ensures consistency across all components and screen sizes.

- **Grid:** A 12-column system is preferred for desktop, collapsing to 4 columns for mobile.
- **Gutters:** Standard 16px (md) gutters ensure sufficient breathing room between content blocks.
- **Margins:** 24px (lg) outer margins provide a safe area for content on smaller screens.
- **Rhythm:** Vertical spacing should strictly adhere to multiples of the 8px base unit to maintain a predictable visual cadence.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is conveyed through tonal layering and soft, ambient shadows rather than harsh borders.

- **Tonal Layers:** Using the neutral palette to create surface containers that sit subtly above the background.
- **Shadows:** Low-opacity, diffused shadows (e.g., `0px 4px 12px rgba(140, 112, 111, 0.1)`) are used to lift interactive elements like cards and menus.
- **Depth Levels:**
  - **Level 0 (Base):** The main background surface.
  - **Level 1 (Raised):** Cards and content modules.
  - **Level 2 (Floating):** Navigation bars and dropdowns.
  - **Level 3 (Overlay):** Modals and dialogs.

## Shapes
The design system has transitioned from sharp, 90-degree angles to a more approachable, rounded aesthetic.

- **Standard Radius:** 0.5rem (8px) for buttons, input fields, and small components.
- **Large Radius:** 1rem (16px) for cards, modals, and container elements.
- **Extra Large Radius:** 1.5rem (24px) for prominent featured sections or floating action buttons.

## Components
- **Buttons:** Feature 8px corner radii. Primary buttons use the Crimson Primary (#eb1237) with white text. Secondary buttons use an outline of the Secondary color.
- **Chips:** Highly rounded (pill-shaped) with subtle backgrounds derived from the neutral or primary palette.
- **Input Fields:** 8px rounded corners with a 1px border using the neutral-light palette, focusing on a 2px Crimson border when active.
- **Cards:** Utilize 16px corner radii and a Level 1 elevation shadow to distinguish themselves from the background.
- **Lists:** Clean, separated by subtle neutral dividers with 8px padding on list items for better touch targets and visual clarity.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Adopt the primary color for selected states, moving away from sharp squares to 4px rounded squares for checkboxes and full circles for radios.
